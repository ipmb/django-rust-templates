  `flat us`/`loop us` are paired medians, so `loop-flat` is a difference of
  two medians and `per-iteration` divides it by n. Both templates render the
  same bytes at every n (control above), so the difference is the loop's own
  control: push, bind the loop variable, update `forloop`, render, pop.

  A per-iteration figure that does NOT fall as n grows is a fixed per-render
  cost being divided by n rather than a per-iteration cost; a figure that is
  flat across counts is the per-iteration cost the design has to beat.
