reading -- `overlay applies=True` means every node in that body is one the
floor handles in Rust, so the loop variable can live in a Rust-side scope and
cost no crossing per iteration. One unhandled node anywhere in the body (the
census is nested) and the variable must be pushed for that node, at which
point the saving is gone. So the fork's ~23% arm is weighted by the fraction
of bodies printing True, and that fraction moves with Rust's coverage --
which is why the class names are printed and the weight is not.
