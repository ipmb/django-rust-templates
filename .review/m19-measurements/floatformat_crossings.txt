`floatformat` is 4 crossings whatever *value* it is handed and 4 with
the argument absent, so the four are the body's own round-trips and not
the input path.  They are the four host calls the body makes
(`decimal_parse`, `decimal_quantize`, `number_format`, `mark_safe`).
A *quoted* argument makes it 5: the extra one is `to_int`, which Rust
answers for a bare number (`Value::Int`) and not for a `SafeString`.
`upper` is 0 because Rust computes it; `lower`/`escape`/`add` are 1.

The delta is +1 on every one: the quoted spelling pays a `to_int` the
